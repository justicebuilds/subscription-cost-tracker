CAPSTONE PLAN

Problem: I have a lot of monthly subscriptions and I need to find out the total I spend on subscriptions combined each month and total for the year on all subscriptions.

Questions: 
What order? Any order
What will be included? Names of subscriptions and monthly cost
Should we include the yearly cost next to the monthly cost? yes
Should we do yearly cost of all subscriptions at the end? Yes

Input: 1. List subscriptions and monthly cost 2. add them up combined for the month total  3. Multiply each subscription by 12 for the yearly cost 4. Multiply each monthly total by 12 for the yearly cost of all subscriptions. 5. Print every subscription, and monthly and yearly cost for each subscription and all subscriptions combined.

Psuedocode: FOR EACH subscription in the list
PRINT the name and monthly cost
ADD the monthly cost to the monthly total
MULTIPLY the monthly cost by 12
PRINT the yearly cost of each subscription
MULTIPLY the monthly total by 12 for yearly total of all subscriptions combined
PRINT the monthly total of all subscriptions
PRINT the yearly total of all subscriptions

Test cases: Disney+, monthly: $13.87, yearly: $166.44
                   Amazon Prime: monthly: $16, yearly: $192
